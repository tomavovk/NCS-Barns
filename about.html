Our Story

Family owned & operated since 1993

What started with a few young Martin boys has grown into three companies providing income and work for about forty area residents.

Luke, Titus & Reuben Martin
Owners & Operators, North Country Storage Barns

We would like to express our thankfulness for your support since 1993. Our journey of a quality product delivered and served with excellence has made us what we are today. We thank you for your patronage and our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ for his guidance at work and in our homes.

In 1993, North Country Storage Barns started as a family-owned and operated shed business. We started out as a very small operation with a few siblings within the Martin family. It was a rough road and a large undertaking in the Northern New York area. Over time, locations were added to widen the reach of the company. Today, we have three locations in New York State and service dealers across the Saint Lawrence River in Canada.

Through the years, some of the Martins have left the immediate area to pursue other aspirations. Luke, Titus, and Reuben still reside in the immediate area and own and operate North Country Storage Barns.

The ever-increasing need for quality-built sheds has caused us to expand our workforce. Now over 30 years later, we have a wide range of product offerings — and the sheds are still manufactured with the same mark of excellence as they were at our inception. Materials have changed and better tools have improved production speed, but the quality you have come to rely upon has not changed.

Furniture, gifts, modular homes, and swing sets have now become a huge part of the company. These products are handcrafted by Amish craftsmen in the Lancaster County, PA area and parts of Ohio. If you visit, you'll be able to take a stroll outdoors to look through our many sheds, vehicle storage units, gazebos, playsets, outdoor furniture, birdhouses & feeders, lawn ornaments, and more!

Inside all three of our locations, you can browse through and order from any of our many catalogs if you don't see just the perfect item in the showroom. Feel free to sit down with a friendly salesperson who's ready to guide you through designing your next custom-made structure!
