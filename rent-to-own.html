About the Program

Rent-to-Own
Program

24–60 Month Rental Terms
$15,000 Maximum Available
1st Month + Security Deposit Down
150 mi from Philadelphia, NY

From sheds to playsets, we offer a variety of rental terms to fit your budget. Rent-to-Own is available for structures up to $15,000, with 24, 36, 48, and 60 month plans.

  • Pay out the contract early at any time for a significant savings.
  • Put only the first month's rent and a security deposit down — building delivered straight to your backyard.
  • Early purchase option available to close out the contract early at a significant discount.
  • Financial hardship? Simply pay rent through the day of pickup — no further obligation.
  • Payments are due exactly 30 days from your structure's delivery date.
Delivery must be within NY State and within 150 miles of Philadelphia, NY. By filling out the application below, you are acknowledging interest in renting from North Country Storage Barns.
