NCS Barns offers free delivery within 60 miles of our three New York State locations — Philadelphia, Yorkville, and Sandy Creek. Deliveries beyond 60 miles are available at an additional per-mile charge.

Our professional delivery team handles everything: transport, placement on your site, and leveling. Most deliveries are completed within 3–7 business days of order confirmation.

  • Free delivery within 60 miles of any NCS location
  • Professional placement and leveling included
  • 3–7 business day lead time after confirmation
  • Site preparation guidance provided at no charge
  • Relocation services available for existing structures

Questions about your delivery area? Call us at (315) 642-0209 or visit any of our three locations.
