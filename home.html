Our Story

30+ Years of Quality, Built for the North Country

Since 1993, North Country Storage Barns has been a family-owned operation dedicated to quality-built structures. Luke, Titus, and Reuben Martin still own and operate the business — serving customers across New York State and beyond. Our furniture and gifts are handcrafted by Amish craftsmen in Lancaster County, PA.
